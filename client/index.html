About Bad Blue

Bad Blue is a nationwide, AI-powered platform built to simplify law enforcement accountability and make legal tools affordable for everyone. It helps users draft and submit police misconduct complaints, file Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) and public-records requests, and assemble Section 1983 civil-rights documentation with AI-driven accuracy.

Users can generate petitions demanding officer accountability, perform officer record searches, and access case-strength analysis via advanced legal AI suggestions. Bad Blue performs much of the legwork automatically, letting citizens focus on justice, not bureaucracy.

Bad Blue is not a law firm, does not offer legal advice, and does not represent users. Its tools are for information and document-preparation only.

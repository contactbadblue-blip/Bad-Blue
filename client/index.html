About Bad Blue

Bad Blue is a nationwide, AI-powered legal information and document-support platform built to make police accountability practical, convenient, and affordable for individuals, advocates, and professionals in major U.S. cities and beyond. The platform helps users generate professional police misconduct complaints and excessive-force reports, route them to the proper internal affairs or oversight agency, prepare structured documentation for potential Section 1983 civil-rights actions, and create clear written records that support accountability.

Using guided prompts and higher-order AI reasoning, Bad Blue assists users in organizing facts, evaluating the apparent strength of a potential claim based on the information they provide, and automatically assembling consistent, legally formatted documents. Users can also generate FOIA and public record requests for body-worn camera footage, incident reports, CAD logs, disciplinary histories, and other law enforcement records; search for and document officer information; and create targeted petitions demanding officer accountability or resignation where misconduct appears substantiated. Bad Blue is designed to handle as much of the legwork as possible while keeping users in full control.

Bad Blue is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not represent users in court. All tools and outputs are provided for informational and document-preparation purposes only. Users are responsible for reviewing all documents, complying with filing rules and deadlines, and consulting a licensed attorney where legal advice or representation is needed.
